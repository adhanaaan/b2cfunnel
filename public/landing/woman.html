You are not imagining it

Hormones are part of the story. They are not the whole story.

Word-finding slips, fuzzy focus, poor sleep, and feeling easily overwhelmed can have more than one driver.

Sleep and mood changes

Hot flashes, poor sleep quality, and mood changes such as anxiety or depression can impair memory consolidation and attention.

Hormonal shifts

Frequently causing brain fog, memory lapses, and attention deficits.

Midlife health

Conditions like diabetes and hypertension can increase cognitive risk, linking brain health with metabolism, blood pressure, and vascular function.

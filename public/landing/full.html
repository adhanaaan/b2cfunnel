Why it matters

Cognitive decline can start from 40.

By the time forgetfulness or early dementia shows up, the window to act has usually narrowed. The earlier you see it, the more you can do.

Your brain health is shaped by everyday, modifiable factors - many of them changeable once you know where you stand.

  • Blood pressure & diabetes - vascular drivers of decline.
  • Diet & exercise - lifestyle levers you can act on today.
  • Family history - know your baseline risk early.

Knowing gives you options.

The solution

A digital brain assessment with 90% accuracy

ReCOGnAIze tells you what's actually happening in your brain. It's an assessment validated against MRI scans, built on a five-year NTU study of 1,500 people and published in Alzheimer's & Dementia.

The science

Built on medical research.
Validated against real brain scans.

ReCOGnAIze comes out of the Dementia Research Centre at NTU LKCMedicine. It was trained on 1,500 people studied over three to five years, using brain MRI, cognitive testing, and blood biomarkers. In a separate 235-person study, its scores were checked against what MRI and clinical assessment showed — published in Alzheimer's & Dementia, a leading peer-reviewed journal in cognitive research.

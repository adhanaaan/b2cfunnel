What we measure

A complete picture of your cognitive health

Most checkups never look at your brain until something goes wrong. We test the biomarkers that actually drive long-term cognition — and translate them into a plan you can act on.

🧠

Cognitive Performance

Validated assessments of memory, processing speed, attention, and executive function — tracked over time.

🫀

Cardiovascular & Metabolic

ApoB, lipid panel, glucose, HbA1c, and insulin — the heart-brain markers that quietly shape cognition.

🔥

Inflammation & Immunity

hs-CRP, homocysteine, and immune markers linked to neuroinflammation and brain aging.

🐟

Nutrients & Omega-3

Omega-3 Index, Vitamin D, B12, folate, magnesium — the building blocks your neurons depend on.

Genetics & Risk
